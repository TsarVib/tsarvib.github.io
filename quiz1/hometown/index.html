Surabaya

Surabaya is the capital of East Java, located on the northeastern coast of Java. As a major port city, it has a long history of trade and continues to be an important center of business and education in Indonesia.

The city is known as Kota Pahlawan, or the City of Heroes. This identity reflects the courage of its people during the Battle of Surabaya on 10 November 1945, an important moment in Indonesia's struggle for independence.

Surabaya brings together historic neighborhoods and modern city life. Its streets, local markets, and public spaces offer different ways to experience the city, while its heritage remains an important part of its character.
